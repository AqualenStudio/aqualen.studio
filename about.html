OUR APPROACH

Depth over noise.

Aqualen Studio is an independent game studio under Joytoart Gaming Ltd. in Cyprus, EU.

We build stylized worlds shaped by restraint, silence, and depth. We are drawn to eastern-inspired mystery, deliberate design, and games that carry a voice of their own.

Our current focus is Stellum. Inkighter is available on Steam, and our earlier experiments remain part of the studio’s story.
